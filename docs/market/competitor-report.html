Where Makeborne stands against Lovable, Bolt, Gamma and the rest

Twenty products across three markets: AI app builders, AI website builders, and AI deck and book tools. Prices and limits come from each vendor's own pricing page or docs as of 13 October 2026, with a few marked third-party figures.

Prepared for Kerim. Read-only research; no accounts created. Makeborne figures come from its own code.

The short version

  • Site limits were our biggest self-inflicted gap. Lovable, Replit, Base44, Bolt, Canva Pro, Durable and Hostinger don't cap how many sites you publish. Framer and Webflow charge per site, and that is their most-cited complaint. Makeborne advertised 1, 5 and 15 sites, while the code actually allowed 5 for everyone.
  • The entry price is in line ($29 vs $20–25), but the free entry points are missing. No free tier, no daily free credits, no rollover, and the badge stays on even when you pay.
  • Our clearest edge is one brand across website, deck and book, plus client work. No competitor does all three formats in one style, and none of the app builders has client CRM, review links and an enquiry inbox. Lovable has no white-label or client billing at all.
  • The most common complaint about every competitor is paying for the AI's own mistakes. Makeborne already doesn't charge for unconfirmed usage. We should say so loudly and guarantee it.
✓
Changed today: unlimited websites on every plan. The 5-live-site and 20-site caps are gone. A quiet fair-use guard stops abuse only: 50 new sites per workspace per day, and a ceiling of 1,000 live sites. Pricing and publishing copy now say "Unlimited websites". Tested locally; not deployed yet.

What a credit really buys

Lovable's published examples: a small edit costs 0.5 credits, adding sign-in 1.2, a landing page with images about 2. On the $25 plan that is roughly 50 landing pages, at $0.25 per credit.

A Makeborne credit is smaller ($0.03 of AI cost, sold at about $0.097 on Create). A medium website build is capped at 50 credits and a light one at 20. We charge only what is measured, but we don't know a typical build's charge yet, because no paid production builds have run. If a typical medium build lands near its cap, $29 buys about 6 full builds, which would compare badly with Lovable. Before launch, measure typical charges per action and publish a simple table, the way Lovable does. Pricing that people can predict is the second most common thing users ask for, after not paying for failures.

What customers complain about elsewhere

App builders

  • Credits burned in bug-fix loops, with "done" claims that weren't true (Lovable, Bolt, Base44, Replit, v0)
  • Bills they could not predict before prompting
  • No refunds for the AI's mistakes; slow support
  • Trustpilot: Bolt ≈1.4, Base44 ≈2.3–2.8, Lovable 4.2

Website builders

  • Per-site costs that stack (Framer, Webflow)
  • Renewal jumps of 300–400% (Wix, Hostinger)
  • Freelancers paying twice: a workspace plan plus a site plan
  • No CRM or lead inbox on design-led builders

Decks and books

  • Opaque credits and hard stops (Gamma, Canva)
  • Custom domains on sites but not decks (Gamma)
  • No tool makes a complete print package: illustrations, bleed, spine and cover together
  • Tome shut down and deleted users' decks

What to do, in order

These are proposals. Pricing and policy are your decisions; the engineering cost is noted on each.

  1. DoneUnlimited websites with a fair-use guard, on every plan.
  2. PricingRemove the "Made with Makeborne" badge on every paid plan. Every competitor does this at $20–25. Small change: the badge is one flag in the site Worker.
  3. PricingLet unused credits roll over for 2 months, as Lovable, Bolt and v0 do. The credit-lot system already tracks expiry per lot, so this is a parameter change plus copy.
  4. TrustPublish a "you never pay for failures" guarantee and a per-action credit table. It directly answers the top complaint across all competitors, and the spending gate already behaves this way.
  5. BuildSelf-service custom domains on every paid plan, ideally with domain purchase. The isolated hosting Worker is ready; this needs Cloudflare custom hostnames and a settings screen. Medium effort.
  6. PricingRethink seats. Lovable has unlimited members sharing one credit pool; Framer and Webflow charge $20–39 per editor. A middle path: unlimited client reviewers and viewers on every plan, and paid seats only for builders.
  7. BuildSite analytics (visits, sources, top pages). Visits are already served by our own Worker, so we can count them without a third-party script. Medium effort.
  8. WedgeAgency tools nobody in AI building has. White-label client portal, client billing with markup, one-click handoff to the client's account. Lovable has none; Wix and 10Web prove agencies pay for it. Builds on our CRM and review links.
  9. WedgeThe complete print-ready book package. Consistent illustrations, interior with bleed, spine width from page count, full-wrap cover and EPUB in one go. The research found no tool doing all of it. Needs AI images (blocked on the OpenAI key).
  10. LaterBackend and payments in generated sites (logins, database, Stripe). This is Lovable's and Base44's core strength. Large effort, and not our wedge; do it after the client and book features.
  11. GrowthA free way in. Every major competitor has one. Even 30 credits a month or 5 a day, with the badge on, would let people reach the "aha" before paying.

Corrections we found in our own product

  • The pricing page promised 1, 5 and 15 sites, but the database allowed 5 live and 20 total for everyone. Fixed by going unlimited.
  • "Expanded hosting" was listed as a Scale feature, but nothing on Scale is different for hosting. Plans now differ by credits, seats and storage, which is honest.
