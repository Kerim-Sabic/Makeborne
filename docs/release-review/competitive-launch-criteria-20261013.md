# Competitive launch criteria

Reviewed 2026-10-13. This narrows the existing competitor research into release decisions. Published features are not a design-quality benchmark; no competitor output comparison or superiority claim is established here.

## Release acceptance, in execution order

1. **Payment correctness:** qualified purchase and credit-pack delivery, duplicate prevention, monthly annual allocations and expiry, transparent reconciliation failures, and a proven provider-supported plan-switch path. A direct provider read does not prove signed webhook delivery or app entitlements.
2. **Public customer output:** use the exact staged publisher and immutable qualified runtime images. Check public HTTPS, mobile navigation, forms arriving in the correct client's inbox, address changes, workspace isolation, and recovery after interruption. Local codec and SDK tests alone do not qualify cloud execution.
3. **Useful generated work:** three different website businesses, a coherent short ebook and an editable presentation. Judge readability, business-specific imagery/layout, accurate inclusions/prices, complete calls to action, mobile behavior and exports. No brackets for unknown contact information. Preserve original outputs and defects in evidence.
4. **Predictable editing:** a narrowly requested change preserves unrelated pages/slides, theme, assets and notes. Resume interrupted work without silent duplicate charges. Review links must identify the exact version and expose only intended client content.
5. **Exact candidate:** fresh staging signup/confirmation/social sign-in/uploads/exports and the reviewed migration/recovery path. Pin source, migrations, app deployment and hosting worker together; old passing evidence does not qualify changed code.

## Differentiation to prove

The strongest current candidate is one client brief carried through website, ebook, presentation, review and delivery in the CRM. This is a product hypothesis, not a measured market advantage. Validate it with complete client projects and side-by-side output assessment; do not promise "best in the world" from a feature checklist.

## Required follow-on scope

Customer custom domains, useful analytics, repository import/two-way sync and generated backend services remain meaningful competitive gaps. Record their real implementation status rather than presenting them as already available. They should not displace the required payment, publication and image-generation gates without an explicit owner scope decision.

No provider generation calls, purchases, deployment or production changes were made for this research.
