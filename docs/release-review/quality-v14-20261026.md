Judged on typography, text accuracy, layout intent, not looking like AI output, and purposeful image placement.

### Deck 1 — Alba Bakehouse (16:9, High, 2560x1440) vs a_deck1–4: at reference level
- All six slides full images; five passed the text gate first time, one regenerated once (stray card numerals), none fell back.

- Shortfalls: the wordmark was set in serif, sans and tracked capitals on different slides (prompt fixed: one face, size and spacing for every slide). Slide 5 repeats "€48,000" and "19 months" in the title and in its cards (designer copy choice). The cover is darker and moodier than a_deck1's split cream field — a different but valid interpretation.

### Deck 2 — Helio (4:3, High, 2048x1536) vs r_solar / r_mars: strong after one fix
- Run 1: five of six full images. The data slide passed with the wordmark printed twice (top and footer) — the vision transcript missed the repetition. The "ask" slide failed twice because the prompt set the accent words in the accent colour on an accent ground (yellow on yellow), and its editable fallback on the 4:3 page showed flat strips above and below the plate and a stack of overlapping scrims.
- Fixes (offline, then only this deck re-run): accent words on an accent ground take the readable deep or light colour; the check asks for footers, corner labels and every repetition; plates fill taller pages and touching scrims merge into one panel.
- Run 2: six of six full images, all first time, one consistent wordmark. Remaining shortfalls: on slide 3 the "%" of "38%" runs into the yellow sun disc (the gate passed it); slide 5's Morning/Afternoon/Evening timeline is the designer's dramatisation of "an install takes 1 day", not a fact from the brief (the claims gate checks figures and status words, not narrative). r_mars's deliberate left/right split is bolder than our cover.

### Book 1 — Mushrooms of the Northern Woods (6x9, High, 1536x2304) vs r_fungi: at reference level
- The cover (regenerated once) and both chapter openers passed the gate: cream serif with gold italic accents, gold rules, precise botanical watercolour on forest green — the reference's language.
- Shortfalls: r_fungi is a landscape specimen plate with eight species; ours is a portrait cover around one chanterelle cluster (the brief asked for a 6x9 book, so the format differs by design, but it has less variety). The opener labels differ in case ("CHAPTER ONE" tracked caps vs "Chapter Two"): the label's case should be fixed in the art system.

### Book 2 — Ollie Was Afraid of the Water (square, High; spread 2560x1280, cover 1920x1920) vs r_whale / r_cat: at reference level
