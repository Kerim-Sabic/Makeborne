## Known conflicts between implementation, terms and owner decisions (not resolved here)

These are recorded, not decided. No commercial policy was changed by this candidate.

1. **Credit-pack expiry.** The durable implementation creates *non-expiring* top-up lots (`docs/CREDIT_TOPUPS.md` line 17). The published terms page does not mention packs at all; the billing FAQ says "Credit packs keep the terms shown when you buy them … Final pricing, expiry and refund terms will be shown before purchase." Pack-specific terms are unpublished and unapproved. Packs are closed, so nothing is sold under conflicting terms today. Owner decision required before packs open.
2. **Plan switching.** The owner requires plan switching at launch (handoff, 2026-10-13); no qualified app tier-switch flow exists (audit L06). The terms describe renewal and cancellation but not upgrades/downgrades. Owner decision: launch with switching handled through Whop support only (and say so), or block launch on L06.
3. **Compiler unavailability.** `feature/launch-core` deliberately made the finish-time compiler fail open ("Builds still save after the static checks", admin System tab). That preserves work but reports the run as a passed project check, which conflicts with I3/I4. This candidate keeps the work-preserving behaviour and changes only the reporting (finding N1 in `FINDINGS.md`).
4. **"Print-ready" books.** The book prompt promises print-ready output while samples fail paperback preflight (audit D01). Whether launch sells paperback output is an owner scope decision; the candidate must not claim it either way without evidence.
