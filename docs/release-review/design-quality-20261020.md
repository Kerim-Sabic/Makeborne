## How the sites were generated

The isolated release-auth stack lacks four migrations that `6b78269` needs, so `run.mjs --live` refuses; changing the shared stack was not permitted. With the coordinator's go, every new site was generated by **direct agent runs** (`scripts/eval/direct-run.mjs`): the app's own `runBuilder` with the same system prompt, tools, model (`claude-opus-5-5`), finish-time static, quality and compile checks and design-review rubric, driven like the studio's Create mode (build, up to two continuations, one design review captured with the studio's own capture code), without the database or the Next server. Each brief reserves its worst case in the eval ledger; every call is bounded by `RunSpend`; unfinished stages are kept only if they compile, as in `website-run.ts`. **These are not app-route runs.** A short app-route confirmation run should follow once the migrations are applied.

- Medium baseline: `golden-20261018` (app routes; the builder prompt is identical in `6b78269`), re-measured for 7 briefs with the current harness (`dq-golden-remeasure-20261020`), plus direct-agent baselines on unmodified `6b78269` code (`dq-base-medium-20261020`, 3 briefs; `dq-base-high-20261020`, 2 briefs). The direct baselines reproduce the golden look (same archetypes and pairings for w07 and w12), so the method does not change the outcome.
- After: `dq-after1-*` (round 1), `dq-after2-medium-20261020` (all 20 briefs, round 2), `dq-after3-medium-20261021` and `dq-after4-medium-20261021` (6 and 4 briefs after further fixes), `dq-images-medium-20261021` (4 briefs with image routing on).
- All sets measured with `measure.mjs --lighthouse-runs 3` (outputs under `outputs/eval/measure/<set>/`).

## Visual judgement

Two reviews of before/after pairs (desktop first screen, desktop full page, mobile full page):

- **My review** (positions shuffled; not fully blind, since I had seen the golden sites): round 1 preferred the new version on 5 of 6 Medium pairs (lost w19, where the seed gave a wedding planner nightlife type and a photo-placeholder box) and lost the High w01 pair (a generic heavy grotesk). After the fixes, round 3 preferred the new version on 5 of 6 (lost w15 on an overflow at 360 px).
- **Claude spot-judge** (blind, both orders, the fixed rubric's instructions verbatim; same model family as the builder, so used only for before/after of Claude output): round 1, new version 5 of 14 verdicts; round 3, 3 of 12; round 4 (4 briefs after the first-screen rule), 4 of 8, winning both orders on w03 and w19 and losing both on w07 and w12. It consistently rewards brief fit made visible (a giant phone number for the plumber, the price placeholder and "illustrative" labels for the SaaS beta, a "checkout not connected" notice for the roastery) and reads dark grounds with gold or neon accents as template-like.

## Honest assessment

Now strong: measurable quality (performance, CLS, contrast, accessibility contracts) and variety across sites, which was the golden set's weakest area; motion that never hides content; forms and pages that pass keyboard and screen-reader basics by construction; with images on, first screens that look commissioned (photographs and cut-outs in the site's palette, honest captions).

Not there yet: visual preference is contested. A blind judge still prefers the calmer golden version on about half the briefs, mostly where a quiet editorial look or one dominant fact (phone, price) serves the brief better than a bolder art direction. Some seeded choices drift toward dark grounds and heavy display type. LCP with a generated hero image is about 4 s on the mobile lab profile (no responsive `srcset`, and the hosted snapshot drops `fetchpriority`); without images it is about 2.8 s, still above the 2.5 s claim bar because React and fonts load before the first paint completes. Lighthouse claim (≥ 95) is not reached. Residual CLS (three sites at 0.10–0.12 on one load of twelve) comes from display headlines re-wrapping when the web font arrives.

## Limitations and rollback

- Direct-agent runs, not app routes (see above). No live competitor comparison and no human designer panel.
- The Claude judge shares the builder's model family; it is a spot check, not the J1/J2 gate.
- Roll back with `git revert` of the branch's commits; nothing was deployed and no data or configuration outside this branch and `outputs/eval/` changed. The eval ledger's cap raise is recorded in `capApprovals` and cannot be undone without a hand edit (the older harness refuses the raised ledger until merged).
